# KICK'S approved design baseline

Status: locked by product owner on 2026-08-19.

KICK'S is a product of DataStorm Inc. It is not a separate company. The official product and app name is `KICK'S`.

The approved visual baseline is:

- black, high-contrast canvas;
- fire-orange and amber brand energy, with electric-blue signal accents;
- the approved ring mascot imagery;
- the current mobile-first card layout and bottom navigation;
- Activity, Engine, Permissions, Opportunities, and Wallet as the primary product areas;
- a prominent, dedicated Exfiltration Engine experience.

Changes to this visual language, navigation model, product name, or DataStorm relationship require explicit product-owner approval. Accessibility, responsive fixes, security corrections, and truthful status labels may be improved without changing the approved identity.
