# Product scope

## Core journeys

1. See recent app-to-company communications and a plain-language commercial explanation.
2. Grant, deny, or revoke a specific permission with purpose, recipient, data categories, compensation, duration, and policy version.
3. Review eligible compensated opportunities and accept only after consent and identity gates pass.
4. View pending, available, and paid rewards with an auditable transaction history.

## Launch-strengthening controls

- KICK'S-owned identity subject mapping; MFA required before sensitive actions.
- KYC provider integration behind an adapter; no fail-open behavior.
- Append-only consent and reward ledgers; corrections use compensating entries.
- Server-side authorization for tenant, owner, purpose, object, and lifecycle state.
- Structured logs, metrics, alerts, trace/correlation IDs, incident runbooks, and restore tests.
- Android traffic monitoring developed as a separately reviewed native capability.

## Non-goals for the 30-day MVP

No business/government portal, scoring, referrals, social feed, dynamic pricing, broad marketplace, behavioral profiling, sensitive-attribute inference, automated regulatory advice, or real-money production payout before launch gates pass.
